# Tiro public beta 7

This beta makes the Wireframe Board easier to keep nearby and share from.

- Pin the board when you want it to stay visible, or let it hide automatically when focus moves away.
- Export a tight square around the artwork, or append its saved path to the text already on your clipboard.
- Use distinct mobile, browser, and dotted frames, visible tool labels, Clear all, and the new Wireframe Board menu shortcut.
